# UAnansi noBulumko

##
Kudala-dala abantu babengazi nto. Babengakwazi ukulima izityalo, ukuluka amalaphu, okanye ukwenza izixhobo zentsimbi. Uthixo uNyame owayesemazulwini wayenabo bonke ubulumko behlabathi. Wayebugcina ngokukhuselekileyo embizeni yodongwe.

##
Ngenye imini, uNyame wagqiba kwelokuba anike uAnansi imbiza yobulumko. Rhoqo xa uAnansi ejonga ngaphakathi embizeni yodongwe, wayefunda into entsha. Yayivuyisa kakhulu loo nto!

##
UAnansi onengcinga zomona wacinga wathi, “Ndiza kuyigcina imbiza ikhuselekile phezulu emthini omde. Emva koko ndiza kuba nayo yonke le nto ndedwa!”

Wajija umsonto omde, wawujikeleza imbiza yodongwe, waza wayibophelela esiswini sakhe. Waqalisa ukunyuka emthini. Kodwa kwakunzima ukunyuka emthini kuba imbiza yayisoloko imbetha emadolweni.

##
Ngalo lonke elo xesha, unyana omncinci kaAnansi wayemi emazantsi emthini ebukele. Wathi, “Bekungayi kuba lula na ukunyuka ukuba ubunokuyibophelela imbiza emqolo wakho?”

UAnansi wazama ukubophelela imbiza yodongwe eyayizele bubulumko emqolo wakhe, yaye ngokwenene kwaba lula kakhulu ukunyuka.

##
Kungekudala wafika encotsheni yomthi. Kodwa emva koko wema waza wacinga wathi, “Bekufanele ukuba ndim onebubulumko bonke, kodwa nanku unyana wam ekrelekrele kunam!”

UAnansi wacaphuka kakhulu ngenxa yoku kangangokuba wayiphosa imbiza yodongwe phantsi emthini.

##
Yaphuka yaba ziziqwengana xa ibetha phantsi. Ubulumko baba bukhululekile ukuba wonke umntu abelane ngabo. Yiloo ndlela abantu abafunda ngayo ukulima, ukuluka amalaphu, ukwenza izixhobo zentsimbi, kunye nazo zonke ezinye izinto abantu abazaziyo ukuzenza.
