# Iculo lika Sakima

##
USakima wayehlala nabazali bakhe kunye nosisi wakhe oneminyaka emini. Babehlala kumhlaba wendoda esisityebi. Uronta wabo ofulelwe ngengca wawusekupheleni kokelo lwemithi.

##
Xa wayeneminyaka emithathu uSakima, wagula waze waba yimfama. USakima wayeyinkwenkwe enekhono.

##
USakima wayesenza izinto ezininzi amanye amakhwenkwe aneminyaka esithupha ayengazenzi. Umzekelo, wayekwazi ukuhlala nabantu abadala belali yakhe baze baxoxe ngemiba ebalulekileyo.

##
Abazali baSakima babesebenza kumzi wendoda esisityebi. Babehamba ekhayangentsasa baze babuye emalanga. USakima wayesala kunye nosisi wakhe omncani.

##
USakima wayethanda ukucula amaculo. Ngenye imini umama wakhe wambuza, "Uwafunda phi la maculo, Sakima?"

##
Waphendula uSakima, "Ayeza nje mama. Ndiyaweva kwintloko yam ndibe sendicula."

##
USakima wayethanda ukuculela usisi wakhe omncani, ikakhulukazi, xa eziva elambile. Usisi wakhe wayemphulaphula ecula elona culo lakhe alithandayo.

##
"Cela uphinde ulicule kwakhona Sakima," usisi wakhe wayemcenga. USakima wayevuma kwaye acule futhi ngokuphinda-phinda.

##
Ngenye imalanga xa abazali babo babebuyile ekhaya, babethule kakhulu. USakima wayesazi ukuba kukhona into engalunganga.

##
"Yintoni engakunganga, mama, tata?" USakima wafunda ukuba unyana wendoda esisityebi wayelahlekile. Indoda yayiphatheke kabi kwaye ililolo.

##
"Ndingamculela. Angaphinda onwabe futhi," uSakima waxelela abazali bakhe. "Unendyebo enkulu. Ungumfana nje oyimfama. Ucinga ukuba iculo lakho liza kumnceda?"

##
Nangona kunjalo, uSakima akazange anikezele. Usisi wakhe omcinane wayemxhasa. Wathi, " Amacula kaSakima ayandithuthuzela xa ndilambile. Azakuyithuthuzela nayo indoda esisityebi."

##
Ngemini elandelayo, uSakima wacela usisi wakhe omcinane ukuba amkhokele kumzi wendoda esisityebi.

##
Wema ngezantsi kwefesitile enkulu waze waqala wacula elona culo lakhe alithandayo. Kancani kancani, intloko yendoda esisistyebi yaqala ukuvela kulo fesitile inkulu.

##
Abasebenzi bayeka lonto babeyenza. Bamamela iculo lika Sakima elimnandi. Kodwa yathi enye indoda, "Alekho umntu okhe akwazi ukuthuthuzela umphathi. Ingaba le nkwenkwe iyimfama icinga ukuba ingamthuthuzela?"

##
USakima wagqiba ukuculq iculo lakhe waze wajika ehamba. Kodwa indoda esisityebi yakhawuleza yaphuma yaze yathi, " Cela ucule kwakhona."

##
Kwangelo Xesha, amadoda amabini aphuma ephethe umntu ngepele. Babesele bemfumene unyana wendoda esisityebi ebethiwe waze washiywa ecaleni komgaqo.

##
Indoda esisityebi yayivuya kakhulu ukubona unyana wayo kwakhona. Wahomba uSakima ngokumduduza. Wathatha unyana wakhe kunye noSakima waya esibhedlele ukwenzela uSakima ezophonda abone futhi.
