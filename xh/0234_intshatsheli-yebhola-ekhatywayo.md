# Intshatsheli Yebhola Ekhatywayo

##
UAndiswa wayebukele amakhwenkwe edlala ibhola ekhatywayo. Wayenqwenela ukuba abe yinxalenye yeqela labo. Wabuza umqeqeshi ukuba angaziqeqesha kunye nawo na.

##
Umqeqeshi wabeka izandla zakhe esinqeni. “Kwesi sikolo, ngamakhwenkwe kuphela avumelekileyo ukudlala ibhola ekhatywayo,” watsho.

##
Amakhwenkwe amxelela ukuba makaye kudlala ibhola yomnyazi. Athi ibhola yomnyazi yeyamantombazana, ize ibhola ekhatywayo ibe yeyamakhwenkwe. UAndiswa waba buhlungu.

##
Ngosuku olulandelayo, isikolo sasino mdlalo omkhulu webhola ekhatywayo. Umqeqeshi wayenexhala kuba umdlali wakhe obalaseleyo wayengaphilanga kwaye engakwazi ukudlala.

##
UAndiswa wabaleka waya kumqeqeshi waza wamcenga ukuba amvumele adlale. Umqeqeshi wayengaqinisekanga ukuba makenze ntoni. Emva koko wagqiba kwelokuba uAndiswa abe yinxalenye yeqela.

##
Umdlalo wawunzima. Akukho mntu wayefake inqaku ngexesha lekhefu.

##
Kwisiqingatha sesibini somdlalo, omnye wamakhwenkwe wayigqithisela ibhola kuAndiswa. Wabaleka ngokukhawuleza esiya epalini. Wayikhaba ngamandla ibhola waza wafaka inqaku.

##
Isihlwele savuya kakhulu. Ukususela ngaloo mini, namantombazana avunyelwa ukuba adlale ibhola ekhatywayo esikolweni.
