# Iibhanana zikaMakhulu

##
Igadi kaMakhulu yayintle kakhulu. Yayizele ngamazimba, amazimba amancinci kunye necassava. Kodwa eyona nto endandiyithanda kakhulu yayiziibhanana. Nangona uMakhulu wayenabazukulwana abaninzi, ndandisazi ukuba mna wayendithanda kakhulu. Wayedla ngokundimema ukuba ndiye endlwini yakhe. Wayendixelela neemfihlo zakhe ezincinci. Kodwa kukho imfihlo enye awayengandixeleli yona: wayezivuthisa phi iibhanana zakhe.

##
Ngenye imini, ndabona ibhaskithi enkulu eyenziwe ngeendiza ibekwe elangeni ngaphandle kwendlu kaMakhulu. Ndambuza ukuba yeyantoni. Wathi, “Yibhaskithi yam yomlingo.” Ecaleni kwebhaskithi kwakukho amagqabi ebhanana awayewajika ngamanye amaxesha. Ndaba nomdla. Ndambuza, “Makhulu, ngawantoni la magqabi?” Wathi, “Ngamagqabi akhe omlingo.”

##
Kwakumnandi kakhulu ukubukela uMakhulu, iibhanana, amagqabi ebhanana kunye nebhaskithi enkulu. Kodwa uMakhulu wandithuma ukuba ndiye kumama. Ndathi, “Makhulu, ndicela ukubukela xa ulungiselela iibhanana…” Wathi, “Mntwana, musa ukuba nenkani. Yenza le nto endikuxelela yona.” Ndakhawuleza ndabaleka ndaya kumama.

##
Xa ndibuya, uMakhulu uhleli ngaphandle. Kodwa ibhaskithi neebhanana zazingasekho. Ndabuza, “Makhulu, iphi ibhaskithi? Ziphi zonke iibhanana? Kwaye iphi…” Kodwa waphendula wathi, “Zikwindawo yam yomlingo.” Ndaphoxeka kakhulu!

##
Kwiintsuku ezimbini kamva, uMakhulu wandithuma ukuba ndiye kumthathela intonga yakhe yokuhamba kwigumbi lakhe lokulala. Ndithe xa ndivula ucango, ndeva ivumba elimnandi leebhanana ezazivuthwa. Kwigumbi elingaphakathi kwakukho ibhaskithi enkulu kaMakhulu yomlingo. Yayifihlwe kakuhle phantsi kwengubo endala. Ndayiphakamisa ingubo ndaza ndeva elo vumba limnandi lweebhanana.

##
Ilizwi likaMakhulu landothusa xa wandibizayo wathi, “Wenza ntoni? Khawuleza undiphathele intonga.” Ndakhawuleza ndaphuma nentonga yakhe. UMakhulu wandibuza, “Kutheni uncumile?” Umbuzo wakhe wandenza ndabona ukuba ndisancumile kuba ndandifumene indawo yakhe yomlingo.

##
Ngosuku olulandelayo, xa uMakhulu wayeze kutyelela umama, ndabaleka ndaya endlwini yakhe. Ndandifuna ukuphinda ndijonge iibhanana. Ndabona iqela leebhanana ezazisele zivuthwe kakhulu. Ndathatha ibhanana enye ndayifihla phantsi kwelokhwe yam. Emva koko ndaphinda ndayigquma ibhaskithi. Ndaya emva kwendlu ndaza ndayitya ngokukhawuleza. Yayiyeyona bhanana imnandi endakha ndayitya!

##
Ngosuku olulandelayo, uMakhulu wayesegadini echola imifuno. Ndangena endlwini yakhe ngokufihlakeleyo ndaza ndajonga iibhanana. Phantse zonke zazisele zivuthiwe. Andizange ndikwazi ukuzibamba, ndathatha iqela leebhanana ezine. Ndithe xa ndihamba kancinci ndisiya emnyango, ndeva uMakhulu ekhohlela ngaphandle. Ndakhawuleza ndazifihla iibhanana phantsi kwelokhwe yam ndaza ndadlula kuye.

##
Ngosuku olulandelayo yayilusuku lwentengiso. UMakhulu wavuka kwakusasa. Wayesoloko ethatha iibhanana ezivuthiweyo necassava ayokuzithengisa emarikeni. Mna andizange ndikhawuleze ndiye kuye ngaloo mini. Kodwa andizange ndikwazi ukumhlala kude ixesha elide.

##
Kamva ngaloo ngokuhlwa, ndabizwa ngumama, noutata kunye noMakhulu. Ndandisazi ukuba kutheni. Ngobo busuku, xa ndandilele, ndandisazi ukuba andisayi kuphinda ndibe. Andisayi kuphinda ndibe kuMakhulu, kubazali bam okanye komnye umntu.
