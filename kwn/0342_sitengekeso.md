# Sitengekeso

##
Ezuva limwe onane kwa kere noyikwenyango yoyinzi.

##
"Siruwoke natu gwana ko nose yikwenyango yimwe" Tatu pura. "Ose tatu gwana yikwenyango yetu naina masiku." Yimo vana kutanta onane.

##
Mumbyange Rahim makurulya. Ta makagere yikwenyango nayinye. Age ta li po yoyinzi.

##
"Tara eyi ana rugana Rahim" Yimo ana kuzigira mumbyange gomunona. “Rahim utwini age hena manyami," Yimo nina kuuyunga.

##
Onane vana handukire Rahim.

##
Nose tuna mu handukire Rahim, nye age Rahim nombili si.

##
“Nomu mu pa sitengekeso Rahim?” Yimo ana kupura mumbyendigona.

##
“Rahim, no lifira nkenda ntaantani, “ yimo vana kumurondora onane.

##
Rahim ta vareke kuvera.

##
"Mezimo lyange kuna kukora" Yimo ana kuwowota Rahim.

##
Onane vana yi diva asi eyi yiyo nayi ka horoka. Yikwenyango yiyo yina kutengeka Rahim.

##
Konyima, Rahim ta gava mbili kwetu. "Kapi hena ngani yi tengwira kukara makurulya," Ta tumbwidire. Makura natuvenye tatu yi pura.
