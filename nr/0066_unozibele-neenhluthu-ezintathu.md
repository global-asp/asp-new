# UNozibele neenhluthu ezintathu

##
Kudala, abentazana abathathu baphuma ayokutheza iinkuni.

##
Bekulilanga elitjhisako yeke behlela emlanjeni ukuyobhukuda. Badlala begodu bafafaza begodu babhukuda emanzini.

##
Msinyana, babona ukuthi besele kuhlwile. Baphuthuma babuyela ngemakhaya.

##
Bathi sebeseduze nokufika ekhaya, uNozibele wabeka isandla sakhe entanyeni. Bekakhohliwe umgexo wakhe! "Ngibawa ubuyele nami!" wancenga abangani bakhe. Kodwana abangani bakhe bathi besele kuhlwile.

##
Ngalokho uNozibele wabuyela emlanjeni yedwa. Wathola umgexo wakhe waphuthumela ekhaya. Kodwana walahleka ebumnyameni.

##
Kude wabona ukukhanya okuvela endlini yotjani. Waphuthuma waya kiyo begodu wangqongqoza emnyango.

##
Wamangala, inja yavula umnyango yathi, "Ufunani?" "Ngilahlekile ngidinga indawo yokulala" kutjho uNozibele. "Ngena, kungenjalo ngizokuluma!" Kutjho inja. Wangena ke uNozibele.

##
Bese inja yathi, "Ngiphekela!" "Kodwana angikaze ngiphekele inja ngaphambilini," waphendula. "Pheka, kungenjalo ngizokuluma!" Kutjho inja. Ngalokho uNozibele waphekela inja ukudla.

##
Bese inja yathi, "Ngilungisela umbhede!" Waphendula uNozibele wathi, "Angikaze ngenzele inja umbhede." "Lungisa umbhede, kungenjalo ngizokuluma!" kwatjho inja. Nje-ke uNozibele walungisa umbhede.

##
Qobe langa bekufuze apheke begodu athanyele begodu ahlanze inja. Kwathi ngelinye ilanga inja yathi, "Nozibele, namhlanjesi kufanele ngivakatjhele abanye abangani. Tjhanyela indlu, upheke ukudla begodu ugezise izinto zami ngaphambi kobana ngibuye."

##
Kwathi nasele ikhambile inja, uNozibele wathatha iinhluthu ezintathu ehloko yakhe. Wabeka ihluthu elilodwa ngaphasi kombhede, enye ngemva komnyango, enye ngaphakathi kwe-kraal. Bese wagijima waya ekhaya ngebelo elikhulu.

##
Kwathi nakubuya inja, yazuma uNozibele. "Nozibele, ukuphi?" Wamemeza. "Ngilapha, ngaphasi kombhede," kwatjho iinhluthu zokuthoma. "Ngilapha, ngemva komnyango," kwatjho iinhluthu zesibili. "Ngilapha, e-kraal," kwatjho iinhluthu zesithathu.

##
Kusekela lapho inja yazi ukuthi uNozibele umkhohlisile. Ngalokho wagijima wagijima indlela yoke eya edorobheni. Kodwana abafowabo bakaNozibele bebalindile lapho bephethe iintonga ezinkulu. Inja yaphenduka yabaleka begodu azange ibonakale kusukela lapho.
