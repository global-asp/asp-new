# Mbuti, Mbyana na Homu

##
Mbuti, Mbyana na Homu avari vanghana lavankulu. Siku rinwani vateke rendzo hi xibazana.

##
Kute loko vafika laha rendzo ravona rihelelaka kona, muchayeri ukomberile leswaku vahakela mali yavona. Homu uve ahakela mali yayena.

##
Mbyana uve ahakela mali yohindzisa hikuva anga khomangi mali leyi ayi laveka.

##
Muchayeri alava kunyiketa Mbyana cici yayena loko Mbuti a suka a tsutsuma angasi hakela nchumu.

##
Muchayeri a hlundzukile swinene. Uve a chayela xibazani anga nyikangi Mbyana cici yayena.

##
Hikokwalaho na namutlha, Mbyana a tsutsumela movheni kuya hlometela a ndzeni naswona ukuma muchayeri loyi a nwi kolotaka cici yayena.

##
Mbuti wa tsutsuma loko atwa mpfumawulo wa movha. Uchava leswaku uta khomiwa hikuva anga hakelangi mali yayena ya rendzo.

##
Kambe Homu angana xiphiqo loko movha wuri nkarhi wu ta. Homu uteka nkarhi wayena loko a tsemekanya patu hikuva u hakele mali yayena yihelerile.
