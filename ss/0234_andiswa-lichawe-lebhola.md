# Andiswa lichawe lebhola

##
Andiswa bekabukela bafana badlala libhola letinyawo. Bekafisa kuhlanganyela kanye nabo. Wacela umceceshi kuth ucela kuticecesha kanye nabo.

##
Umceceshi wabeka tandla etingculwini/ekhalweni."Kuleskolo lesi, kuvumeleke bafana kuphela kudlala libhola letinyawo," kwasho umceceshi.

##
Bafana bamtjela kuth akahambe ayodlala libhola letandla. Bathi libhola letandla lemantfombatane bese libhola letinyawo lebafana. Andiswa kwamphatha kabi loku.

##
Ngelilanga lelilandzelayo,sikolo besinemdlalo lomkhulu. Umceceshi bekakhatsatekile ngobe umdlala wakhe lodlala kahle bekagula futh bekangeke akhone kudlala.

##
Andiswa wagijimela kumceceshi wamncenga kuth amvunele adlale. Umceceshi bekanganaso siciniseko sekuth ente njan. Watsatsa sincumo sekuth Andiswa angahlanganyela lelicembu.

##
Umdlalo bewumatima. Kute bekasafake lincaku ngesiwombe wokucala.

##
Ngesiwombe sesibili semdlalo munye webafana wakhahlelela ibhola ku Andiswa. Wasuka ngekukhulu kuphatima wagijimela epalini. Wakhahlela ibhola kakhulu washaya lincaku.

##
Sicuku sagcwala injabulo. Kusukela ngalelo langa, emantfombatane awunyelwa kuth adlale libhola letinyawo esikolweni.
