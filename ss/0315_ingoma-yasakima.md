# Ingoma yaSakima

##
Sakima bekahlala nebatali bakhe kanye nadzadzewabo loneminyaka lemine. Bebahlala eveni lendvodza lenjingile. Lendlu yabo lefulelwe ngetjani beyisekugcineni kwemgca wetihlahla.

##
Ngesikhatsi Sakima aneminyaka lemitsatfu, wagula futsi wangasaboni kahle. Sakima bekangumfana lonelikhono.

##
Sakima bekenta tintfo letinyenti lebetingentiwa ngulabanye bafana labaneminyaka lesitfupha. Sibonela saloko kutsi bekangahlala nemalunga lamadzala asendzaweni futsi acoce ngetintfo letibalulekile.

##
Batali baSakima bebasebenta endlini yalendvodza lenjingile. Bebahamba ekhaya ekuseni kakhulu bese babuya nasekuhlwile. Sakima wasala nadzadzewabo lomncane.

##
Sakima bekatsandza kuhlabela tingoma. Ngalelinye lilanga make wakhe wambuta watsi, "Utifundzaphi letingoma Sakima?"

##
Sakima waphendvula watsi, "Tivele tifike make. Ngitiva enhloko yami bese ngiyahlabela."

##
Sakima bekatsandza kuhlabelela dzadzewabo lomncane, ikakhulukati nangabe ativa alambile. Dzadzewabo bekamlalela ahlabelela ingoma yakhe layitsandzako. Bekatawugucugucuka ngendlela lepholisako.

##
"Ungaphindzaphindza yini kuyihlabela, Sakima," dzadzewabo bekamncenga. Sakima bekayemukela futsi ayihlabele aphindze ayiphindze.

##
Ngalelinye lilanga kusihlwa ngesikhatsi batali bakhe babuyela ekhaya, bebathulile kakhulu. Sakima bekati kutsi kukhona lokungahambi kahle.

##
"Yini inkinga make, babe?" Kubuta Sakima. Sakima weva kutsi indvodzana yalendvodza lenjingile ayilahlekile. Lendvodza beyidzabuke kakhulu futsi inesitunge.

##
"Ngingamhlabelela. Kungenteka aphindze ajabule," Sakima watjela batali bakhe. Kodvwa batali bakhe bamcosha. "Unjinge kakhulu. Ungumfana loyimphumphutse nje. Ucabanga kutsi ingoma yakho itamsita yini?"

##
Nanobe kunjalo, Sakima akazange aphele emandla. Dzadzewabo lomncane wamsekela. Watsi, "Tingoma taSakima tiyangipholisa uma ngilambile. Titawupholisa nendvodza lenjingile."

##
Ngelilanga lelilandzelako, Sakima wacela dzadzewabo lomncane kutsi amholele endlini yalendvodza lenjingile.

##
Wama ngephansi kwelifasitelo linye lelikhulu futsi wacala kuhlabela ingoma yakhe layitsandzako. Kancane kancane, inhloko yalendvodza lenjingile yacala kuvela ngelifasitelo lelikhulu.

##
Tisebenti tayekela loko lebetikwenta. Balalela ingoma lemnandzi yaSakima. Kepha lenye indvodza yatsi: "Kute lokhonile kudvudvuta umphatsi. Lomfana loyimphumphutse ucabanga kutsi utamdvudvuta yini?"

##
Sakima wacedza kuhlabela ingoma yakhe wagucuka wahamba. Kodvwa lendvodza lenjingile yaphuma ngekushesha yatsi: "Ngicela uphindze uhlabelele."

##
Ngaso leso sikhatsi, kwefika emadvodza lamabili aphetse umuntfu lotsite ngesitulo. Bebatfole indvodzana yalendvodza lenjingile ishaywe futsi ishiywe eceleni kwemgwaco.

##
Lendvodza lenjingile yajabula kakhulu ngekuphindze ibone indvodzana yayo. Wavuza Sakima ngekumdvudvuta. Watsatsa indvodzana yakhe naSakima wabayisa esibhedlela kute Sakima aphindze abone.
